📡 Core Telemetry & Navigation
High-Visibility Address Stack Auto-Shrink
Instant reverse-geocoding displays full street numbers, abbreviations (St., Ave., Rd.), town, state code, and county zip. Dynamic font sizing keeps long names from overflowing.
Google Maps Link Sharing Single Tap
Tap the top Location Card to copy a direct Google Maps GPS coordinate link to your clipboard with an instant confirmation badge.
Telemetry Multi-Badge Tap Badge
Cycles between Heading & Bearing (e.g. 270° W), GPS Altitude in feet, and real-time GPS Accuracy Margin (±ft).
Heading-Up vs. North-Up Compass Tap Compass
Toggle between active forward-driving orientation with needle tracking (lower third camera sightline) and locked True-North perspective.
Dual Highway Shield Concurrency Automatic
Intelligently renders simultaneous Federal Interstate shields and State Route badges (e.g., I-95 & Route 128 / I-93 & Route 3) while snapping directional headers to your line of travel.
⚡ Dash Customization & Weather Engine
High-Contrast Speed Sign Tap Speed
Authentic highway regulatory sign styling. Tap to instantly toggle between MPH and KM/H.
Map Styles (Dark / Neutral) Tap Vehicle
Tap the vehicle marker to alternate between CARTO Dark Matter (night mode) and Positron (high-visibility neutral day mode).
Live Weather & UV Telemetry Tap Temp
Real-time conditions from Open-Meteo including dynamic weather icons, relative humidity percentage, and color-graded UV index warnings. Tap temperature to toggle between °F and °C.
Weather Refresh Configuration Hold Temp (500ms)
Long-press temperature to set your custom update polling: Time intervals (10m, 20m, 30m, 60m) or Distance triggers (15mi, 30mi, 50mi, or Off).
Fullscreen & Screen Wake Lock Tap Top Icon
Locks your phone display awake during long road trips and triggers native borderless immersive fullscreen navigation.
